THE NAME

Because everything else changes.

The job changes. The paycheck changes. The knees, the schedule, the year — all of it moves. The bar weighed this much the day you started, and it’ll weigh this much the day your kid graduates. That’s not cruelty. That’s the only fair deal you’ll get all day. Iron never changes. You do.
