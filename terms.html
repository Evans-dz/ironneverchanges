Who we are

Iron Never Changes is a heavyweight gym wear brand. We sell in the United States, through this site, www.ironneverchanges.com. When this page says “we” or “us,” that’s Iron Never Changes. “You” is you.

When you order from us or use this site, you’re agreeing to these terms.

02

The shirts

The line is 28 shirts. Each one comes in sizes S, M, L, XL, 2X and 3X, and in four garment-dyed washes: washed black, blue, brown and grey.

Garment dye means the wash varies a little from shirt to shirt, and from the photos on this site. Yours may not match the picture exactly, or the next guy’s. That comes with the dye.

03

Prices and payment

Prices are per shirt, in US dollars, and shown on each shirt in the shop. Prices can change. The price shown at checkout is the price you pay.

Checkout runs on Stripe Checkout, a payment page hosted by Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe and never touch this site. Stripe collects your email, name, card and US shipping address so the order can be paid for and sent to you.

Until you check out, your cart is kept in your own browser, not with us. One order can hold up to 24 lines (a line is one shirt in one size and wash), with up to 10 of each line.

04

Shipping

We ship to US addresses only. Shipping is a flat $6 per order, however many shirts are in it, and it’s added at checkout.

05

All sales final

All sales are final. We don’t take returns or exchanges, so check the size and the wash before you order.

One exception, and we mean it. If an item arrives damaged, or it isn’t what you ordered, email ironneverchanges@gmail.com with your order details and a photo. We’ll make it right with a replacement or a refund.

Coaching is with Zac. It’s $100 a month, month to month, with no contract. Cancel any time by telling Zac.

Coaching isn’t sold through the shirt checkout. You set it up with Zac directly: email ironneverchanges@gmail.com, or reach him from The Corner on the home page.
