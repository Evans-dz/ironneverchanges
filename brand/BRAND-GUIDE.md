# IRON NEVER CHANGES — Logo Package

*Where limits meet defeat.*

Everything here was rebuilt as true vector art from your original logo, so it scales
from a hat patch to a gym wall without going soft. Black, white, and grey only —
plus a vintage/distressed set for the worn-in look.

**File types**

- `.svg` — vector. Send this to printers, embroiderers, and sign shops. Infinite scale, no quality loss. All type is already converted to outlines, so nobody needs your fonts.
- `_4000px.png` — transparent, print resolution (13"+ at 300 DPI). For DTG, DTF, and anyone who says "just send a PNG."
- `_1000px.png` — transparent, for web, social, and email.

Every asset comes in `_black`, `_white`, and `_grey`. Use white on dark garments — don't
just print the black one and hope.

## Rules that actually matter

**Clear space.** Keep empty space around the logo equal to the height of the skull.
Nothing crowds it — no text, no borders, no other logos.

**Minimum size.** Full badge: 1 inch / 100px wide. Below that the slogan turns to mush —
use the mark alone or the one-line wordmark instead.

**Don't:** stretch it, rotate it (except the spine art, which is built for it),
add gradients, add drop shadows, outline it, recolor it outside the three brand colors,
or put the black version on a dark shirt.

**Do:** let it go big. This mark is built for an 11–14" back print.

## Sending art to a printer

- **Screen printing** — send the `.svg`. Tell them 1 color (black or white). This is the cheapest and most durable option, and it's what makes the vintage look work.
- **DTF / DTG** — send the `_4000px.png`. Confirm they're printing at 300 DPI at final size.
- **Embroidery** — send the `.svg` of the **mark** only. Embroidery can't hold the fine barbell knurling or the small slogan type; expect to pay a one-time digitizing fee (usually $25–75).
- **Vintage/distressed** — send the files from `06_VINTAGE-DISTRESSED`. Warn the shop the texture is intentional so nobody "cleans it up" for you.

## Which art goes where on a shirt

| Placement | Use | Typical size |
|---|---|---|
| Left chest | `mark-skull-barbell` or `lockup-horizontal-oneline` | 3.5–4" wide |
| Full front | `wordmark-stacked` or `badge-primary` | 10–12" wide |
| Full back | `badge-primary` or `wordmark-stacked` | 11–14" wide |
| Spine (center back) | anything in `05_SPINE-BACK-PRINT` | 2.5–3" wide × 14–18" tall, starting ~3" below the collar |
| Sleeve | `tagline-bar` or `mark` | 1.5–2.5" wide |
| Hem / bottom back | `tagline-bar` | 4–6" wide |
